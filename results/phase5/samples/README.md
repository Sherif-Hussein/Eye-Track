# Raw-signal samples for the dataset figure

Generated by `scripts/phase5_sample_data.py` (re-runnable, about 2 minutes). Data only, no charts. Aggregate or
sample signals only: no stimulus images, no names, no celebrity or parent identities. Internal participant IDs
appear below and in `selection_log.json` for traceability; do not publish them (use the anonymised labels).

Selection rules were fixed before looking at any trace and are stated per file. Ties are broken by lower ID.

## 1. Delmas (EyeLink 1000, 1000 Hz, famous-face CIT, 2 x 2 face display)

Files: `delmas_trial_1000hz.csv` (4,999 rows), `delmas_trial_30hz.csv` (150 rows), `delmas_layout.json`.

Selection: group 2 (concealment, no countermeasure; the group-1 memory-only participants and groups 3-5 with
countermeasures are excluded). All 15 group-2 participants were screened (each has a single main-session EDF and
an authors' matrix file; trial alignment between the EDF and the authors' response matrix was checked from the
inter-onset intervals, max disagreement under 5 ms). Candidate trials: parallel display containing the known
(famous) face, correct response, fewer than 5% of the 5 s display samples missing (gaze or pupil). 386 of 960
screened trials qualified. Chosen: the candidate whose response time to the probe image (987 ms) is closest to the
pooled candidate median (986.5 ms), i.e. the 50th percentile; missing samples 0.0%. The 5 s parallel display has
fixed duration for every trial, so response time was used as the "typicality" measure. Caveat: the chosen trial
is the participant's first trial of the session.

Columns (both CSVs):
- `time_ms`: ms from parallel-display onset (0 = onset). 1000 Hz file: 0 to 4998 ms in 1 ms steps. 30 Hz file: 0 to
  4966.7 ms in 33.33 ms steps. The window stops one sample short of 5000 ms to stay under the 5,000-row limit.
- `x_px`, `y_px`: gaze position in screen pixels, 1920 x 1080 screen, origin top-left, x right, y down.
- `pupil_au`: EyeLink pupil area, arbitrary units (as recorded; not baseline corrected).
- `velocity_deg_s`: gaze speed used by I-VT (central difference over +-4 ms, in deg/s; 34.99 px/deg).
- `ivt_event`: `fixation`, `saccade` or `missing`. From the project's I-VT (`edd.delmas_raw.ivt`): velocity
  below 30 deg/s and a run of at least 60 ms is a fixation. `saccade` here means every non-fixation sample (it
  includes sub-60 ms runs and samples next to gaps), not only samples above the velocity threshold. There are no
  missing samples in this trial.
- `fixation_id`: running index of the fixation (0-based) or -1 for non-fixation samples.
- `quadrant`: 0 = outside all four face rectangles (or missing), 1 = upper left, 2 = upper right, 3 = lower left,
  4 = lower right; `quadrant_name` gives the same as text. Computed per sample (`edd.delmas_raw.aoi_of`).
- `in_known_face_quadrant`: 1 if the sample lies in the quadrant that held the known face.

Preprocessing: `edd.delmas_raw.load_session` (samples with missing gaze or pupil, or pupil <= 0, set to NaN). The
1000 Hz file is the unprocessed segment. The 30 Hz file is the same trial after `edd.delmas_raw.degrade`: gaps
linearly filled, polyphase anti-alias FIR (Kaiser) `scipy.signal.resample_poly` with up 3 and down 100, output sample marked
missing if any input sample within half an output period was missing. I-VT and quadrant labels are recomputed on
each rate's own samples (30 Hz: same thresholds, so the 60 ms minimum fixation is 2 samples). Fixations: 15 at
1000 Hz, 10 at 30 Hz.

`delmas_layout.json`: screen size, the four face rectangles `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` in px (each 300 x 400 px), quadrant
numbering, px per degree, fixation-cross centre, `known_face_quadrant` (4 = lower right in this trial),
display duration (5000 ms), the response time to the probe image (987 ms), correctness, and
`parallel_display_to_probe_onset_ms` (10,085 ms; the interval between the logged parallel-display time and the
logged single-display time in the authors' matrix, as documented in `Fields_matrices_data.txt`).
Image content is not included.

## 2. Chen et al. (EyeLink, released at 100 Hz, familiar-face moving RSVP)

Files: `chen_pupil_participant.csv` (500 rows), `chen_pupil_group.csv` (750 rows).

Both files use the project's Phase 2 preprocessing (`scripts/phase2_chen.py: load, preprocess`) on the cached
100 Hz traces: pupil blink reconstruction as the authors' (`p_dm`), baseline = mean of the 5 samples (50 ms) before
critical-face onset, subtractive correction, trials with undefined baseline or per-participant baseline |z| > 2
removed, the authors' four excluded participants dropped (Exp 1: 29 participants, 4,011 familiar/control trials
left, of which 1,339 familiar and 2,672 control; the target-face trials are not used). Condition `familiar` = the
participant's parent's face; `control` = the two control-face conditions pooled.

Columns:
- `time_ms`: ms from critical-face onset, 0 to 2490 in 10 ms steps (250 samples at 100 Hz).
- `condition`: `familiar`, `control` (group file also `familiar_minus_control`).
- `mean_pupil_au`: baseline-corrected pupil size, EyeLink arbitrary units (pupil area units).
- `sem_pupil_au`: standard error of the mean. Participant file: across that participant's trials
  (ddof 1, divided by sqrt of the number of trials contributing at that time point). Group file: across
  participants of the per-participant condition means (ddof 1), so it is a between-person SEM; for
  `familiar_minus_control` it is the SEM of the paired per-person difference.
- `n_trials` (participant file): trials contributing at that time point. `n_participants` (group file): 29.

Participant B (subject 5): "complete data" = no missing pupil sample in any retained familiar or control epoch
(11 of 29 participants qualified); chosen is the one whose retained-trial count (140; 48 familiar, 92 control) is
closest to the median of those 11 (140). The group file uses all 29 participants (per-person means computed with
missing samples ignored). Gaze x/y of Chen is not exported (only pupil was requested).

## 3. Celniak et al. (Tobii Pro Fusion, mock-crime CIT, question slides)

File: `celniak_gaze_sample.csv` (2,507 rows). Raw Tobii export as cached in `data/celniak_cache`.

Selection: question slides (names `Q_R`: 7 questions x 3 repetitions) of the 38 recordings sampled at 250 Hz
(798 slides screened; recording "Recording1" is the only 120 Hz one and is excluded). Qualifying slides have
fewer than 5% of samples with no gaze point (247 slides). Chosen: the slide whose missing fraction (2.1%) and
duration (7,572 ms) are closest to the medians of the qualifying slides (2.1%, 7,568 ms).
Participant C belongs to the guilty group (label is internal; not needed for the figure).

Excerpt window: 1.0 s before the slide start to 9.0 s after it (10 s). The question slide itself lasts 7.5 s
(about 7.57 s as logged); the last 1.5 s are the next stimulus. Slide start = recording timestamp
131,837.458 ms (Tobii recording clock); `time_ms` is 0 at that moment.

Columns:
- `time_ms`: ms relative to the slide start (negative = before). Sampling 250 Hz (4 ms steps; mean gaze
  missing rate in the excerpt 1.8%).
- `recording_time_ms`: Tobii recording timestamp in ms (slide start = 131,837.458).
- `slide`: `before`, `question_slide`, `after` (the neighbouring stimuli are not named).
- `x_px`, `y_px`: Tobii gaze point (both eyes combined) in screen pixels, 1920 x 1080 (read from the export's
  recording-resolution fields), origin top-left. Can fall outside the screen. `x_norm`, `y_norm` = px / 1920 and
  px / 1080. Empty where the gaze point is missing (46 samples).
- `valid_left`, `valid_right`: Tobii validity flag per eye (1 = Valid).
- `gaze_missing`: 1 if no gaze point was reported.
- `pupil_mm`: pupil diameter in mm (Tobii "filtered" value, falling back to the mean of left and right when
  absent, as in `edd.celniak._pupil`); empty in 6 samples; not baseline corrected.
- `tobii_event`: Tobii Pro Lab eye-movement classification (`Fixation`, `Saccade`, `Unclassified`,
  `EyesNotFound`).

Preprocessing: none beyond the unit conversion above (no filtering, no resampling, no interpolation).

## Gaps and notes

- Celniak sampling rate: the task brief said about 250 Hz; confirmed from the timestamps, 38 of 39 recordings are
  250 Hz and one is 120 Hz. The module docstring `src/edd/celniak.py` still says 120 Hz.
- Celniak slide start is a recording-clock time; the stimulus names and AOI (item) names are not exported.
- Delmas 1000 Hz file has 4,999 rows (0-4998 ms), not 5,000, to respect the row limit.
- Delmas pupil and Chen pupil are EyeLink area units with no physical calibration; Celniak pupil is in mm.
- The Delmas trial is the first trial of its session (a consequence of the closest-to-median rule, not chosen by eye).
- No MU3D or Liar's Dice data were used.
