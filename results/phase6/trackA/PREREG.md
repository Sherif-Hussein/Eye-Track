## 0. What was known before this document (disclosure)
The Phase 5c reviewers recomputed several of these quantities and quoted them in `docs/review_5c_*.md` (Chen detection at matched FPR +19 to +26 points; Colasanti G2-vs-G3 delta +0.009 after rank averaging; Delmas per-group person AUCs; Delmas known-minus-unknown contrasts at 1000 Hz; Preti RT benchmark AUC about 0.96). `results_posthoc_per_repeat_auc.json` (Phase 4) already holds the per-repeat mean AUC for five tasks. ## 1. Conventions (all analyses)
- Bootstrap: participant-cluster resampling with replacement (a resampled participant brings all of its rows, repeats, groups and arms), B = 2000, `numpy.random.default_rng(seed)`, percentile 95% CI, the same resampled participants for every arm inside one resample. Seeds: A1 20261201, A2 20261202, A3 20261203, A4 20261204, A5 20261205, A6 20261206, A7 20261207, A8 none (analytic). One-sided bootstrap p = (1 + #{delta* <= 0}) / (B + 1), called a descriptive tail probability, never a null-hypothesis p-value (the bootstrap conditions on the fitted models, Stats M1).
- AUC: rank-based, ties by mid-rank, positive class = the class the task calls positive (liar, guilty, known face, familiar).
- "Repeat-averaged" = mean over repeats of a decision unit's score (the Phase 4 pre-registered definition). Frozen folds are used as stored.
- Nothing in Track A is a new confirmatory test. Every p is descriptive unless a family is named. Where a Holm family is used it is stated.

## 2. A1: Chen detection against realised FPR over all thresholds
- Data: the 56 participants (29 + 27). Arms: `rate_rule` and `I2b` (confirmatory checkpoints `repeat{0,1,2}.pkl`, columns `z_fam, z_c1, z_c2`), `unified` (Phase 4 `chen_unified_persons.csv.gz`, `kind` fam / c1 / c2, column `stat`), and the `cascade` (decisions in `chen_cascade_decisions.csv.gz`, binary, so a single operating point, not a curve).
- Statistic per participant and arm: the repeat-mean of z_fam (positive) and of z_c1 (negative). **The control comparison is counted once per participant** (z_c1 = -z_c2 exactly): primary null = z_c1; sensitivities: z_c2, and the original double-counted pool (c1 and c2 both, FPR = their average).
- Curve: empirical ROC (detection = P(z_fam > t) against realised FPR = P(z_null > t) over every threshold t). Table for the figure: detection at realised FPR <= x for x = k/56, k = 1..8 (1.8% to 14.3%), for each arm, with the paired participant-bootstrap band; the curve on a 0 to 0.25 FPR grid with bands; detection and realised FPR of every arm at its own stored calibrated threshold, with c1 counted once.
- Partial AUC: area under the empirical ROC (trapezoid) for FPR in [0, 0.10], reported as pAUC/0.10 (chance 0.05, perfect 1) and as McClish-standardised pAUC (0.5 chance, 1 perfect). Full AUC (c1 once) also reported.
- Paired participant bootstrap (B = 2000, seed 20261201) of: pAUC(I2b) - pAUC(rate rule) [**A1 headline**], pAUC(unified) - pAUC(rate rule), pAUC(unified) - pAUC(I2b); detection differences at each matched FPR; the cascade point against I2b's curve at the cascade's realised FPR.
- Sensitivity: per-repeat curves (each repeat's z used separately, pAUC averaged over the 3 repeats).
- Bonferroni-over-screening bound: the confirmatory one-sided bootstrap p (0.010495, `phase3/chen_confirm/results.json`) times m for m = 9, 11, 13 (13 = the rows of `phase2/chen/ideas_table.csv`; 11 excluding the two baselines; 9 as the reviewer's lower count), capped at 1. The same multipliers are applied to the A1 pAUC p. This is a bound for how fragile the result is to the selection, not a test of the selection.
- Decision rule (stated in advance): "the Chen gain is robust to the operating point" = the paired 95% CI of the pAUC difference I2b - rate rule excludes 0 and the detection difference is positive at every matched FPR in the grid; "survives selection" is NOT claimed in any case, only the Bonferroni-adjusted values are reported.

## 3. A4: Delmas participant-level CIT AUC per group
- Groups and sets: G1 = 64_g1, G2 = 64_g2, G3 = 64_g3 (G3 counted once), G4 = 32_g4, G5 = 32_g5. 32_g3 (the last 32 trials of the same G3 people) is reported as a labelled sensitivity row, never pooled with 64_g3. Each group has 15 participants (G4, G5 saw only the 32-trial window; their window is half as long, a stated limitation).
- Statistic: the Phase 2 / Phase 4 CIT statistic, replicated exactly (`edd.unified._cit_z`): z = (mean known - mean irrelevant) / SD irrelevant of a participant's OOF image scores in a repeat; simulated innocent = same participant, |known| random irrelevant images as pseudo-probes (rng = default_rng(repeat + participant id)). Repeat-average the statistic per participant. AUC per group on 15 known vs 15 simulated innocents. Sensitivity: expected-innocent AUC with 50 independent pseudo-probe draws per participant.
- Models (stored row-level OOF scores, `phase2/delmas_raw/checkpoints/*_oof.csv.gz`): published-table SVM (`book_svm`, 1000 Hz only, authors' label-dependent table), published-model SVM on rebuilt label-free features (`P4_svm_1000`, `P4_svm_30`), ours = `ALLz_lr_1000`, `ALLz_lr_30`; plus the Phase 4 unified pipeline at 30 Hz from `delmas_*_persons.csv.gz` (also its detection and realised FPR at the stored 7% threshold).
- Intervals: participant bootstrap within each group (B = 2000, seed 20261204, same participants for all models). Differences: ours - published at each rate; 30 Hz - 1000 Hz within model. Pooled groups: no countermeasure (G1+G2, n = 30), countermeasure (G3+G4+G5, n = 45), strong countermeasure (G4+G5, n = 30), by stratified bootstrap.
- No decision rule; descriptive with CIs. Read per-group results as rough (n = 15 per group).

## 4. A5: Delmas known-versus-unknown univariate contrasts
- Data: per-presentation rows of `data/derived/delmas_raw_face_trials.csv.gz` joined to the BookFIP `face` label (known = 1) per (participant, image); rows without a label are dropped (as in the track tables). Features per presentation as in `edd.delmas_raw` (dwell_1 = 0.2-1 s, dwell_2 = 1-5 s, first_fix, lat_first; secondary: dwell_b1..b5, n_fix, n_visit, pupil_face). lat_first is as stored (censored at 5000 ms when the face is not fixated).
- Contrast: **within trials that contain a known face** (trials with exactly one quadrant labelled known), per participant: mean over the known-face presentations minus mean over the unknown-face presentations of the same trials (NaN skipped). Sign: known minus unknown.
- Window (primary): the benchmark window per group (64 trials for G1, G2, G3; last 32 trials for G4, G5; G3 once). Sensitivities: (i) the last 32 trials for every group (one common window); (ii) 30 Hz features instead of 1000 Hz; (iii) G3 over its last 32 trials. Primary rate: 1000 Hz.
- Effect size: paired d_z = mean(diff) / SD(diff) over the group's participants, 95% percentile bootstrap CI (B = 2000, seed 20261205), raw mean difference with CI, paired t p (two-sided, descriptive), Holm over the 20 primary contrasts (4 features x 5 groups; descriptive family).
- Group x face interaction: d_z(G1) - d_z(Gk) for k = 2..5, and G1 vs pooled G2-G5 (independent groups, bootstrap within group); also the raw-mean-difference interaction in standardised units (Hedges g of the participant differences).
- Decision rules, stated in advance, for "quick first look, then avoidance by those hiding recognition" against the memory-task account (Delmas et al. attribute late avoidance to memorising unknown faces):
  - The concealment-specific account is SUPPORTED only if, in G2 (concealment, no countermeasure), early orienting is present (first_fix d_z > 0 and lat_first d_z < 0, both CIs excluding 0) AND late avoidance is present (dwell_2 d_z < 0, CI excluding 0) AND the interaction G2 versus G1 is in the predicted direction (G2 stronger orienting or stronger late avoidance than G1, CI excluding 0).
  - The memory-task account is SUPPORTED if G1 (no concealment instruction) shows late avoidance (dwell_2 d_z < 0, CI excluding 0) with d_z not less negative than G2's by a CI-excluding-0 margin, i.e. the interaction CI for late dwell does not show G2 below G1.
  - If neither, "not resolved at n = 15 per group". Both can be true for different features; each feature is judged separately and reported separately.

## 5. A2: sensitivity of the AUC definition (all 7 Phase 4 primary tests)
- Tests: Delmas non-inferiority (mean over six sets of image-level AUC, P4 SVM 1000 Hz baseline), Chen (the primary is detection at FPR <= 7%, which has no AUC definition; the AUC analogues are person AUC and trial AUC, so those two are reported and labelled as the Chen AUC rows), Celniak, Preti A, Preti B, Mazza G2-vs-G3, Mazza guilty-vs-innocent. Baselines exactly as `edd.unified.baseline_unit_scores` / `delmas_primary`; unified scores from `phase4/unified/oof/*_rows.csv.gz`.
- Three estimators per arm: V0 = the pre-registered one (repeat-averaged score, pooled AUC; reproduced and checked against `phase4/unified/results.json`); V1 = per-repeat rank averaging (within each repeat the scores of all decision units pooled in that metric are mid-rank transformed to (0,1], ranks averaged over repeats, pooled AUC; for Delmas the transform is within repeat and set); V2 = mean of per-repeat pooled AUCs.
- Report for each: AUC of both arms, delta (unified - baseline) with participant-bootstrap 95% CI (B = 2000, seed 20261202), and the change of delta against V0. Flag a "sign flip" when the point estimate changes sign or the CI changes from excluding to including 0 (or the reverse).
- Also the descriptive "difference between the two definitions" per arm and task, to correct the "up to 0.02" sentence.

## 6. A3: realised FPR at the nominal 7% threshold
- For every unified-pipeline task in `phase4/unified/oof/*_persons.csv.gz` (Delmas six sets with simulated innocents, Chen (also the confirmatory rate rule and I2b arms), Celniak (+ control variants), Preti A and B (+ contrast-only), Mazza two tasks): realised FPR = share of negatives with stat > tau_fpr7 (tau fixed per fold from training participants only), averaged over repeats within participant, then over negative participants; 95% participant-cluster bootstrap CI (B = 2000, seed 20261203); detection at the same threshold with its CI.
- Threshold rule per task, tabulated: training negatives m, k = floor(0.07 m), tau = the (k+1)-th largest training negative statistic, so the training FPR is at most k/m (this is 0 whenever m < 15). Chen: c1 once (primary), c2, and both.
- Flag: the realised FPR exceeds 7% by more than its half-width of the CI; and whether 7% lies inside the CI. Summary: mean, range over tasks.

## 7. A7: Preti reaction-time benchmark without a model
- Participants from `data/preti/preti2026.xlsx` via `edd.preti` (liar = 1). No model, no fitting, no threshold tuning, no folds.
- Primary score (fixed now): the participant's mean RT (ms) on Unexpected (critical) questions minus the mean RT on Control questions, `RT_U - RT_C`; higher = more likely liar. AUC with participant bootstrap CI (B = 2000, seed 20261207), exact label-permutation p (10000 permutations, descriptive), EER of the empirical ROC (in-sample, descriptive).
- Descriptive alternatives, reported but not primary: `RT_U`, `RT_U - RT_E`, `RT_E - RT_C`, `log RT` contrast, `RT_U / RT_C`.
- Comparison: Phase 4 Level B pipeline (`preti_B_ocular_rt`) and the honest baseline `B_fusion` (OOF repeat-averaged), difference with paired participant bootstrap (the no-model score has no OOF; the comparison therefore favours nothing and is descriptive).
- Per-second ocular result: the no-model contrasts `blink_per_s` (U - C) and `pc_sub` (pupil, U - C) as defined in `edd.preti` (blinks per second of RT, duration-normalised), with the same AUC and CI; and the stored Phase 2 per-second ablation AUC (0.532) quoted from `results/phase2/preti/comparison_card.md`.
- Statement the result is allowed to support: "a one-number RT contrast reaches AUC x [CI]; the fixed pipeline reaches y". It says nothing on recognition tasks.

## 8. A6: power and minimum detectable effect
- For each primary comparison: n participants, n1/n0, both arms' AUCs, the bootstrap correlation between the two AUC estimates, the bootstrap SE of delta (2000 resamples, seed 20261206, from stored scores), the Hanley-McNeil SE of each AUC and of the difference (correlation from the bootstrap), and a binormal simulation (2000 datasets at the actual n1, n0, AUCs and the observed within-class score correlation between arms) of the SD of delta. Headline MDE uses the bootstrap SE: MDE = (z_{1-alpha} + z_{0.80}) x SE for one-sided alpha = .05 and alpha = .05/7 (Holm bound). Chen: SE of the detection difference, MDE in points. The minimum single AUC detectable against 0.5 at 80% power (Hanley-McNeil, one-sided .05) at the actual n1, n0.
- Non-inferiority (Delmas, margin 0.02): power = Phi(margin/SE - z_{1-alpha}) at true delta in {0, -0.005, -0.01}; alpha in {.05, .025, .05/7}; SE from the paired bootstrap of the Phase 4 primary (six-set) and of the five-group estimand (G3 once); n needed for 80% power at true delta 0 (SE scales as 1/sqrt n).
- The SE conditions on the fitted models (it excludes refit variance), so the powers are upper bounds; stated in the output.

## 9. A8: corrected repeated-CV intervals
- For each primary test, the per-fold metric on the test fold at the primary decision level, the same folds for both arms: AUC of the fold's decision units (person tasks; Delmas image-level AUC per set; folds with a single class give a missing value and are dropped, which is stated), detection rate at the FPR <= 7% threshold among the fold's familiar participants (Chen). d_rf = unified - baseline for repeat r, fold f. J = number of (r, f) pairs.
- Nadeau-Bengio corrected variance of the mean difference: (1/J + n_test / n_train) s^2 with s^2 the sample variance (ddof 1) of d_rf over all J pairs, n_test / n_train = the mean over folds of test units / training units. This is also the Bouckaert-Frank variance for repeated k-fold with J = r k (their recommended correction uses n_test/n_train = 1/(k-1)); reported next to the naive (1/J) variance. The interval is mean(d) +/- t_{J-1, .975} sqrt(var); one-sided p from the same t. Delmas: computed per set and combined as the mean of the six sets with independent variances (and with G3 once).
- Also reported: the across-repeat interval (each repeat's mean delta as one observation, t with R - 1 df) as a deliberately conservative check.
- The point estimate of this analysis is the mean of per-fold deltas, which differs from the pooled-AUC delta of Phase 4 (it is a different, per-fold estimand); both are shown.
