## The one change
2.2b code (`lro_common.py`, `s2_sim.py`, `s4_run.py`) imported unchanged. Per axis: tau_fast, tau_slow and the fast component's absolute variance are those of `measured/jitter_model.json`; only the slow component's variance is rescaled so that the process's total (long-run) SD equals the measured residual SD (x 2.0950, y 1.5262 deg). The 2.2b code builds the process as sigma*sqrt(w) (fast) plus sigma*sqrt(1-w) (slow), so the new pair is sigma = measured SD and w = fast variance / measured variance. If the fast variance alone exceeded the target the run would stop (checked in `sa_params.py`: it does not).

(`jitter_params_reduced.json`.) The parameters are passed from outside by wrapping `s2_sim.load_measured` in `sa_run.py`; no 2.2b file is edited. Gains (0.88, 0.79), per-trial offsets, loss, frame times, anti-alias filter, window, thresholds, seeds and folds are exactly those of 2.2b. Because the random streams do not depend on the jitter scale (the same standard normals are drawn), every draw has the same frame times, offsets and losses as in 2.2b; only the jitter amplitude differs, so the comparison with 2.2b is paired.

## Runs
Both routes (dwell from samples, 10 features; I-VT 30 deg/s, 60 ms, 11 features; no pupil), correction none and app, rates 30, 20, 15, 10 Hz, draws 1, 2, 3, the frozen folds and the nested L2 logistic regression of 2.2b (`results/phase9/low_rate/s4_webcam.py` via `s4_run.py`): 4 x 2 x 2 x 3 = 48 nested-CV runs. `sa_run.py cv` is the loop of `s4_run.cv_stage` without the draw-1 regular-timing pipeline check (that check has no jitter and is identical to 2.2b; it is not repeated). The feature stage is `s4_run.feat_stage` as is (it also computes the regular-timing features for draw 1; they are not used). Checkpoints: `features/r<rate>_p<id>.pkl` and `checkpoints/<route>np_obs<none|app>_<rate>Hz_d<draw>*`; both stages skip finished units on a restart. One process, one thread.

## Statistics (`sa_stats.py`)
One call of `results/phase9/low_rate/lr_common.py paired_bootstrap` (unchanged): 2000 participant-cluster resamples, seed 20261102, configurations = clean, simulated measured noise ('anch') and the 2.2b observed conditions (read from `results/phase9/low_rate/checkpoints_webcam` and `results/phase9/low_rate_observed/checkpoints`, read only) plus the new reduced-slow conditions; the same resamples serve all of them, so every difference is paired and the 2.2b rows are reproduced exactly (checked against `rate_table_observed.csv`). AUC = mean over the 3 draws, AUC interval = first 300 resamples, every difference = all 2000. Reported beside the 2.2b values: AUC with interval, change from 30 Hz with interval, one-sided lower bound (5th percentile) and p = (1 + #{change <= -0.02}) / 2001 against the -0.02 margin (no multiplicity correction), dwell minus I-VT with interval, observed(reduced) minus clean at 30 Hz with interval (all rates in the csv), reduced minus 2.2b observed (extra, same rate and correction), EER and HTER (the nested-CV track's image-level and participant-level values, mean over sets and draws).

## Realism checks (`sa_checks.py`)
1. Drift-check failure rate and mean applied correction at 30 and 10 Hz, read from the `diag` stored in the feature files (all 75 participants x 3 draws): failure rate = 1 - share of the 64 trials per participant whose simulated pre-trial correction passed the app's rule (at least 8/15 of the expected valid frames, correction at most 6 deg); mean applied correction = mean over passing trials of the correction magnitude in degrees (trial-weighted over participants and draws). Recorded values: 7% (6 of 85 scored trials) and 2.64 deg (mean magnitude of the 79 passed trials). Computed for 2.2b features and for the reduced features, so the closeness of each simulation to the recordings is stated. The recorded rate is from one person (85 trials), so it has a wide interval (reported, exact binomial).
2. Jitter statistics: the estimator of `s0b_fit_jitter.py` (sigma around each dot's median, lag 1-5 autocorrelations, RMS sample-to-sample) applied to 1000 fresh replicates of the reduced process on the same 156 dots and frame pattern as the measurement (seed 991, as in s0b), against the measured values and against the 2.2b process. `s0b_fit_jitter.py` runs a fit when imported, so its estimator and simulator are re-typed in `sa_checks.py`; the re-typed code is verified by reproducing the stored 2.2b `check_fresh_seed_1000_reps` numbers with the 2.2b parameters. Fit quality is reported as the differences from measured; the reduced process is not re-fitted.
